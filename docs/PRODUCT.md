# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Electronic component buyers — hobbyist makers, professional engineers, and students browsing for parts. They are at a desk, focused on finding the right component for a project. They need to quickly filter by specs, compare alternatives, and understand stock availability.

## Product Purpose

A full-stack electronic components trading platform for browsing, comparing, and ordering electronic parts. Success means users can find the right component in under 30 seconds, compare specs side-by-side, and complete an order without friction.

## Brand Personality

Modern minimal. Clean, fast, premium tool feel. Think Linear or Stripe — confident whitespace, precise typography, no visual noise. The interface should feel like a well-organized parts bin: everything has its place, nothing is decorative.

## Anti-references

- Digi-Key / Mouser — too dense, overwhelming, legacy catalog feel
- Generic SaaS dashboards with gradient cards and emoji icons
- E-commerce templates with cluttered sidebars and promotional banners

## Design Principles

- **Signal over noise** — every element earns its pixel. If it doesn't help the user find or decide, remove it.
- **Data density with breathing room** — show enough information to make decisions, but never crowd.
- **Consistency through system** — shadcn primitives, design tokens, no one-off styles.
- **Match the mockups** — the original screen designs in docs/screens define layout, structure, and information hierarchy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Keyboard navigable. Sufficient color contrast. Reduced motion respected.
